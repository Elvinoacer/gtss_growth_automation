Connect your AI (optional)

GTSS uses Google's free Gemini API to qualify leads and draft outreach messages. The free tier is generous — 15 requests/minute, 1 million tokens/day. You can skip this and add it later in the web app's Settings.

  1. Open Google AI Studio in your browser.
  2. Sign in with any Google account.
  3. Click "Get API key" → "Create API key".
  4. Copy the key (starts with AIza...) and paste it below.
